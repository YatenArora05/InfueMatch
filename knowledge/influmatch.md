# InfluMatch Knowledge Base

## About InfluMatch

InfluMatch is an AI-powered platform that connects brands with influencers for effective marketing collaborations.

The platform simplifies influencer discovery, campaign management, communication, and performance tracking by providing an intuitive interface for both brands and influencers.

InfluMatch is designed to make influencer marketing faster, smarter, and more transparent.

---

InfluMatch provides the following features:

- Secure Authentication
- Brand Dashboard
- Influencer Dashboard
- Campaign Management
- Influencer Discovery
- AI Chat Assistant
- Personalized Recommendations
- Analytics Dashboard
- Profile Verification
- Messaging System
- Notifications
- Collaboration Tracking

# User Roles

There are three types of users.

Brands can:

- Create campaigns
- Edit campaigns
- Delete campaigns
- View applicants
- Shortlist influencers
- Reject applicants
- Track campaign progress
- View campaign analytics
- Manage collaborations

## Influencer

Influencers can:

- Create their profile
- Upload social media details
- Showcase previous collaborations
- Browse campaigns

- Track application status
- View completed collaborations

## Admin

Administrators can:

- Manage users

# Registration

Users can register using their email address.

After registration they should verify their email before accessing all platform features.

---

# Login

Users can log in using their registered email and password.

If login fails:

- Verify credentials
- Reset password
- Contact support if necessary

---

# Password Reset

If a user forgets their password:

1. Click "Forgot Password".
2. Enter the registered email.
3. Receive a password reset link.
4. Create a new password.
5. Login again.

---

# Email Verification

New users receive a verification email after registration.

Until email verification is completed, certain features may remain unavailable.

# Profile Completion

Influencers should complete:

- Profile photo
- Bio
- Categories
- Social media links
- Follower count
- Engagement rate
- Previous collaborations

Brands should complete:

- Company name
- Company description
- Industry
- Website
- Contact information
- Brand logo

A complete profile increases trust and improves matching.

# Campaign Creation

Brands can create campaigns by providing:

- Campaign title
- Campaign description
- Budget
- Category
- Target audience
- Platform
- Deadline
- Deliverables

After submission, the campaign becomes available to eligible influencers.

---

# Campaign Requirements

Each campaign may specify:

- Minimum followers
- Minimum engagement rate
- Content category
- Preferred social media platform
- Geographic location
- Budget range

Only influencers meeting the criteria should apply.

# Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I become an influencer?

Complete your profile and begin applying for campaigns.

Q: Can brands edit campaigns?

Yes, brands can edit campaigns before or during their active period depending on platform rules.

Q: Can I withdraw an application?

Yes, influencers can withdraw applications before acceptance.

Q: Can I delete my account?

Users may request account deletion through account settings or support.

Q: How do I reset my password?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page.

Q: How do I verify my email?

Click the verification link sent to your registered email.
